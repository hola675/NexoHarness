# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith while discussing technical decisions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Accept correction and keep discussions oriented toward evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and other conduct that would make a reasonable contributor feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security or contact channel configured for this repository. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation or take other action needed to keep the community safe.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant style and is intentionally concise for the foundation phase.
